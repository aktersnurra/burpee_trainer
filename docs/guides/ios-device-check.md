# iPhone check: the keyboard dock and the swipes

Chromium cannot show what iOS does with the keyboard or its own gestures, so these are checked on
a phone. Add the app to the Home Screen first (Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen; remove and re-add
an old shortcut, since iOS keeps the launch image per shortcut), then check it opened as an app,
not in Safari, as the keyboard behaves differently in each. Do it once in each.

## Launch

- [ ] The launch screen shows the app icon on the stone background, not the old plank figure.
- [ ] The loader is a dark figure on the page, not in a black square.

## The keyboard dock (Edit, a request in New, the coach)

- [ ] Tapping the box lifts it above the keyboard; it is never covered.
- [ ] Typing several lines grows the box and stays above the keyboard; the last line you wrote stays visible.
- [ ] Dismissing the keyboard (swipe down on the page, or Done) puts the box back at the bottom,
      above the home indicator, with no gap and no jump.
- [ ] Sending, then the reply arriving, keeps the newest message at the top of the screen.
- [ ] Rotating the phone and rotating back leaves the box where it should be.
- [ ] In Safari with its bottom bar visible, the box still clears the bar.

## The swipes

- [ ] Swipe right from the middle of the screen opens the swap list; swipe left closes it.
- [ ] Swipe right from the very left edge: note what happens. If iOS goes back instead, the swap
      list needs a start point away from the edge.
- [ ] Swap, Edit and New sit along the bottom edge clear of the home indicator, and a tap on each
      opens the drawer, Edit or New.
- [ ] In New, holding + or − keeps stepping without selecting text or opening a callout, and stops
      at the value's limit.

## Taps

- [ ] Every button can be hit with a thumb without a miss (Save, Run, the round buttons).
- [ ] The camera toggle beside Home's play is off and disabled when the camera is unavailable.

Report what failed with the iOS version and whether it was the Home Screen app or Safari.
