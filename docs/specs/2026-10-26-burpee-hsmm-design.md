# Burpee HSMM, replayable traces, and a pose corpus

## Status

Approved in conversation on 2026-10-26 ("do 1-4, I want a proper HSMM"). Supersedes the
counting rules of the earlier camera-counting specs (zero-setup HSMM, low-front HSMM,
pushup occlusion and gap tolerance): their thresholds, gates and reset rules are replaced
by the model below. Their invariants stay: no camera path fabricates a rep, and a
rep needs fresh evidence of the return to standing.

## Problem

- The counter was a greedy threshold state machine with special cases added one fix at a
  time (pushup-only dropout tolerance, a 0.47 return threshold, a body-span gate, an
  out-of-order reset, a 500 ms tolerance). Each was tuned until two recordings counted
  exactly.
- One unusable frame mid-rep lost the rep. Dropping 1% of frames cost about 5% of reps.
- The recorder kept only usable frames, so replaying a trace was not what the counter saw
  live, and the fixtures could not show the failure.
- `/tracking-test` ran an older, separate counter, so nothing seen there applied to
  sessions.
- There was no way to measure the counter on more than the two fixtures.

## Design

### 1. One counter

`/tracking-test` (`pose_debug.js`) counts with `stepBurpeeHsmm` and shows its phase and
reps. The legacy decoder stack is deleted: `pose_rep_counter`, `pose_rep_fsm`,
`pose_phase_decoder`, `pose_phase_emissions`, `pose_candidate_extractor`,
`pose_decoder_diagnostics`, with their tests and the page's decoder-diagnostics panel. The
page's 10 s trace recorder stays.

### 2. The HSMM (`pose_burpee_hsmm.mjs`)

**Clock.** The model runs on a fixed 100 ms tick. A tick's evidence is the mean of the
phase log-likelihoods of its usable frames. A tick with no usable frame is a missing
observation: the model advances through its transitions with no evidence. Dropouts and
frame-rate changes need no special case.

**Phases.** `standing → lowering → pushup → rising → standing`, plus `other`
(`standing → other → standing`) for movement that is not a rep: bending, squatting,
adjusting the phone during a rest. Each phase is a chain of `minTicks` sub-states followed
by a self-looping last sub-state, which gives it a minimum duration and a geometric tail
(an explicit-duration HSMM in expanded-state form). `standing` and `pushup` have long
tails: rests and multiple pushups have no cap.

**Emissions.** Per phase, a diagonal Gaussian over the six world features the counter
already uses (`worldBodyVerticalSpan`, `worldHipVerticalSpan`, `worldTorsoElevation`,
`worldWristVerticalSpan`, `dWorldBodyVerticalSpan`, `dWorldWristVerticalSpan`), mixed
with a small per-frame outlier density so one glitched frame cannot dominate.

**Frame usability** is unchanged (`usableBurpeeHsmmFrame`, now exported and shared with
the tracker): full world landmark coverage, confidence ≥ 0.5, macro landmark confidence ≥
0.5, visible fraction ≥ 0.35, finite features.

**Decoding.** Online Viterbi with a fixed lag of 600 ms: each closed tick, the best path
is traced back 6 ticks and that tick's phase is committed. Committed phases are final.

**Counting.** A rep is counted when the committed path returns from `rising` to an
observed `standing` tick, and the cycle since the last standing had observed ticks in at
least two of `lowering`, `pushup`, `rising`. Its time is the first standing tick of the
return. A dropout alone cannot produce a rep; a cycle seen going down and coming back up
with the floor occluded does count.

**API.**

- `initialBurpeeHsmmState(model = BURPEE_HSMM_MODEL)`
- `stepBurpeeHsmm(state, frame) → { state, reps }`: `reps` lists rep times (ms, tracker
  clock) committed by this frame, usually empty, earlier than `frame.tMs` by the lag.
- `flushBurpeeHsmm(state) → { state, reps }`: commits the lagged tail (replay, tests).
- `segmentBurpeeHsmm(frames, model) → [{ tMs, phase, observed }]`: offline Viterbi, for
  training and diagnostics.
- `state.cadenceMs`: all rep times so far.

**Session timing.** `pose-tracker:rep` carries `lagMs` (frame time − rep time). The
session hook dates the rep `lagMs` earlier for its cadence and its work/grace eligibility.
The 2 s final-rep grace covers the 600–700 ms lag.

**Model.** Parameters live in `pose_burpee_hsmm_model.mjs`, generated by
`assets/js/tools/fit_burpee_hsmm.mjs`. Fitting is supervised Viterbi training: segment
each trace with the current model, keep traces whose decoded count equals their known
reps, and refit means, deviations, phase durations and the standing branch rates from the
segmentation. A pass is kept only if at least as many traces count exactly as before it:
on the two fixtures alone a pass collapses the standing deviations and miscounts one, so
it is rejected. The first model was bootstrapped from the previous counter's segmentation
of the two fixtures; refitting needs the corpus.

### 3. Replayable traces (payload version 2)

During a capture segment the tracker records every frame the counter receives, in order:
usable frames as full samples (as before), unusable ones as `{ tMs, usable: false }`, and
a reset of the counter as `{ tMs, reset: true }`. The chunk payload is
`{ version: 2, samples, reps }`, where `reps` are the rep times the counter emitted, carried
by the next chunk flushed. Invariant: replaying a v2 trace's samples (resetting at each
reset marker) through the counter, without a final flush, yields exactly its reps. Version
1 payloads still load; they replay without dropout markers.

### 4. Pose corpus

- `mix burpee_trainer.export_pose_corpus OUT_DIR [--database PATH]`
  (`Workouts.PoseCorpus`) writes one `session-<id>.json.gz` per completed session with a
  completed capture run: logged reps (`reps`), planned reps, program snapshot, camera
  cadence, payload version, the main segment's samples in chunk order, and the reps the
  camera emitted (`camera_reps_ms`). `--database` reads a copied SQLite file (for example
  the production database) instead of the configured one; only the Repo starts.
- `node assets/js/tools/pose_corpus_report.mjs FILE_OR_DIR...` replays each session
  through the current counter and prints, per session and in total: logged, live and
  replayed counts; whether a v2 replay reproduces its live reps; and the replay's reps
  matched to the program schedule (best clock offset, then nearest scheduled rep within
  half an interval): matched, missed, extra. Fixture files load the same way. It exits
  non-zero when a v2 replay does not reproduce its live reps.
- `node assets/js/tools/fit_burpee_hsmm.mjs FILE_OR_DIR... > pose_burpee_hsmm_model.mjs`
  refits the model from fixtures and exports (see Model).

## Verification

- Session fixtures (140 and 135 reps) count exactly; with every 30th frame unusable they
  still count exactly; features rebuilt from keypoints count exactly.
- Unit properties on synthetic 12 fps cycles: a clean cycle counts once; a long dropout
  from standing to standing counts nothing; a pushup occluded for 12 s counts once after
  observed rising and standing; a short dropout while lowering still counts; an `other`
  excursion (squat, bend) counts nothing; halving the frame rate keeps the count.
- Tracker: a controlled fixture's recorded chunks, with dropouts, replay to exactly the
  reps it emitted; unusable frames are traced as markers; a rep event carries its lag.
- Session hook: a rep event with `lagMs` is dated `lagMs` earlier.
- Corpus export: a tracked session's chunks export in chunk order with its metadata.
- Full asset tests and `mix precommit` pass.

## Results at design time

Two fixtures, replay counts (truth 140 / 135), previous counter (with the 500 ms fix) vs
HSMM:

| Condition | Previous | HSMM |
|---|---|---|
| Clean | 140 / 135 | 140 / 135 |
| 20% of frames unusable | 137–139 / 135 | 140–142 / 135–136 |
| 1% bursts of 830 ms | 108–120 / 104–111 | 138–140 / 130–133 |
| 1% bursts of 1.2 s | 103–108 / 95–100 | 134–136 / 126 |
| One frame in three | 129 / 130 | 141 / 136 |

Trained on one fixture and tested on the other: 135 of 135, and 141 of 140.
